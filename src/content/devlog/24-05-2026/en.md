The site is now open as a small personal hub instead of a scattered set of profiles. It brings together projects, links, contact points, stream identity and devlog notes in one place.

This first version is mostly about direction: a clean base, a custom domain, social previews, analytics and enough structure to keep adding work without rebuilding the site every time.

![First public version of the Llyneuf site](/24-05-2026.png)

The first public version already acts as a small base for links, projects and future notes.

I wanted the site to feel personal before it feels complete. The important part is not having every project perfectly finished, but having a place where unfinished work can still be understood.

A lot of the first work was invisible from the outside: domain setup, HTTPS, search console verification, social preview images, analytics and cleanup. It is not flashy, but it makes the site easier to share and easier to grow.

Next steps are focused on making the devlog more useful, improving project pages and turning the site into a place where progress is visible while things are still growing.
