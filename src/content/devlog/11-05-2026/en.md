This experiment started as a simple idea: rebuild my real room as a stylized stream space, then push it through a PSX-inspired visual direction so it feels personal without becoming too literal.

The current work is mostly about blocking out the room, testing shaders and checking whether the environment still reads well once it is imported into Warudo.

I want this kind of background to become part of the stream identity: not just a static scene, but a small world that can change as the channel and character grow.
